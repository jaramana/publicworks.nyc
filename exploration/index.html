Six things to look at before anything is built. Pick one structure and any studies worth folding into it.

C, and its two extremes

  • C — Drawer

    The selected direction. Category tabs stand proud, projects sit filed behind them. No wordmark, no instructions. Arrow keys and type-ahead are silent.

  • C / maximal

    Same structure, every surface made up: grain, glass, bloom, chromatic fringe, an iridescent frame, a specular sweep, parallax.

  • C / zen

    Same structure, nothing drawn that does not have to be. No borders, no glass, no shadows. One hairline on the whole page.

Superseded

  • A — Tabs

    A stack of overlapping sheets. Peeking pulls one sideways out of the drawer.

  • B — Ledger

    A sortable table under one glass sheet.

Interaction studies

  • 1 — Liquid functional layer

    Glass on the pills and the preview panel only. The list underneath stays flat. Sliders for every value.

  • 2 — Haze

    Pointing at a name disturbs the air and the air settles. The displacement runs on a hidden copy, so the readable text never moves.

  • 3 — Wear (wildcard)

    a:visited rewritten as paper. Files you open stand proud, soften at the edge, and collect a faint smudge. Nothing reorders.
