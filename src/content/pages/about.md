## What is here

The Works are the current products. Each one has an About page and a Data page, and each links to its code.

The Archive holds earlier projects, small tools and the maps of Cidade Labs, the sister lab in Galicia. It stays closed until you open it.

## Redux

Redux marks a past professional project rebuilt independently, on the author's own time, from public sources only. NYC Hazard Historian is the first.

## How this page is made

Astro builds the page from one Markdown file per project. The type is PW Heros, a subset of TeX Gyre Heros from the GUST e-foundry, served from this site. The page sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other hosts.
