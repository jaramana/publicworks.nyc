PW Heros
========

PW Heros is a modified version of TeX Gyre Heros 2.004, by B. Jackowski and
J. M. Nowacki for the GUST e-foundry. It is not the original font.

Original package: qhv2.004otf.zip from
https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre/heros/
(downloaded 5 October 2026)

License: the GUST Font License 1.0 (GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt in this folder),
which applies the LaTeX Project Public License 1.3c or later.

Changes made on 5 October 2026 for publicworks.nyc:

- Kept two styles, Regular and Bold.
- Subset to Basic Latin, Latin-1 and common punctuation, dashes, quotes
  and arrows.
- Kept the kern, liga, locl, tnum, lnum and pnum features. Removed hinting.
- Converted to WOFF2.
- Renamed the family to PW Heros, as the license requests for modified files.
  The copyright notice in each file records the change.
